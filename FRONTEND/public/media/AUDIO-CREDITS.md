# Demo audio — not scientific sonification

atlas-demo.wav: original audio created for this project, 10 seconds, mono
22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM. Synthesized overlapping sine/harmonic chimes with attack
and release envelopes; contains no recordings, third-party music, or NASA data.

Bundled locally as the default demo. Its changing pitches and envelopes make
actual audio-reactive frequency bars visible, unlike a steady test tone.
The player identifies the clip and displays an explicit demo label.
