# Background music

“Tranquility” by Kevin MacLeod, from Calming (2014).
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kevin_MacLeod_-_Tranquility.ogg
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Original recording streamed without edits, at reduced playback volume and looped.
Decorative background music; not NASA data sonification.

Requires an internet connection. Playback is attempted immediately on page load. If browser autoplay policy blocks
it, playback retries on the first interaction. The Music ON/OFF button controls
the enabled preference. Music pauses during
chapter audio, and resumes when the chapter audio pauses, stops, or ends.
Future audio integrations should call claimForegroundAudio() and release its
returned function on pause, end, or unmount.
